# Resources: prioritised article ideas

Ideas are ranked by buyer intent: how close the reader is to a sourcing decision, and how well we can answer with real knowledge from our own supply. Write an article only when we can add specific, checkable information. Topics not listed are not a priority.

**Rules for every article:**
- A named author, where there is a real person to credit.
- Sources for factual claims.
- A clear line between general buying guidance and our own confirmed services.
- Never publish minimum orders, prices, availability or scores that we haven't confirmed in writing.
